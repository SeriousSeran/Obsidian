# Project Prioritization Framework

## ICE Scoring

Score each aspect from 1 to 10 to help prioritize product ideas or features.

### Impact (1-10)
How much will this move the needle if it works?

### Confidence (1-10)
How confident are we that this will work?

### Ease (1-10)
How easy is it to build and test this? (10 = very easy, 1 = very hard)

**Total ICE Score:** Impact × Confidence × Ease
