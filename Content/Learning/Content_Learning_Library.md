# Content Learning Library

> Learn writing, editing, and publishing from open, legal resources.

## Core Open Resources

| Resource | Link | Why Use It | Vault Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| OpenStax Writing Guide with Handbook | [OpenStax Writing Guide](https://openstax.org/books/writing-guide/pages/index) | Free legal writing textbook | Build editing checklists |
| Purdue OWL | [Purdue OWL](https://owl.purdue.edu/owl/) | Free writing, citation, and style support | Use for citation and structure questions |
| Writing Commons | [Writing Commons](https://writingcommons.org/about-us) | Open-access writing textbook/resource | Use for composition and revision ideas |
| The Elements of Style | [Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/37134) | Classic public domain writing guide | Baseline sentence structure rules |
| Hemingway Editor | [Hemingway Editor](https://hemingwayapp.com/) | Free writing clarity tool | Editing drafts for readability |

## Paid / Library Books to Learn From Legally

| Book | Legal Link | Best Use |
|---|---|---|
| On Writing Well | [HarperCollins](https://www.harpercollins.com/products/on-writing-well-william-zinsser) | Non-fiction writing principles |
| Show Your Work! | [Austin Kleon](https://austinkleon.com/show-your-work/) | Sharing creative work |
