# Cash Flow Planning Framework

> **Disclaimer:** This content is for planning and educational purposes only. It is not a financial recommendation or a substitute for professional financial advice.

## Core Principles

- **Visibility First:** You cannot manage what you cannot see. Track everything.
- **Buffer Building:** Always prioritize building a liquid cash buffer (runway).
- **Separate Facts from Projections:** Base decisions on current reality, not optimistic future assumptions.

## 1. Income Tracking (Inflows)

- **Reliable Income:** Salary, steady retainer contracts.
- **Variable Income:** Freelance, side projects, product sales.
- *Action:* List all income sources and categorize them by reliability. Calculate a conservative monthly average for variable income.

## 2. Expense Tracking (Outflows)

- **Fixed Essential:** Rent/Mortgage, utilities, insurance, groceries. (Must pay).
- **Variable Essential:** Transportation, basic clothing, essential maintenance.
- **Discretionary:** Dining out, entertainment, subscriptions, hobbies.
- *Action:* Calculate your "Survival Number" (Fixed Essential + minimum Variable Essential).

## 3. The Cash Buffer (Runway)

- **Current Liquid Cash:** $_______
- **Survival Number:** $_______ / month
- **Runway:** (Current Liquid Cash / Survival Number) = _______ months
- *Action:* Set a target runway (e.g., 3 months, 6 months) and divert excess income to hit it.

## 4. Debt Management

- List all debts (Credit cards, loans, mortgages).
- Note the interest rate, minimum payment, and total balance.
- *Action:* Prioritize paying off high-interest debt aggressively after establishing a minimum 1-month cash buffer.

## 5. Investment & Growth (The Upside)

- *Only address this after the cash buffer is full and high-interest debt is eliminated.*
- Retirement accounts, index funds, business reinvestment.
- *Action:* Automate a fixed percentage of income to investments.

## Review Routine

- **Weekly Check-in:** Log expenses, check account balances. (15 mins)
- **Monthly Review:** Reconcile income vs. expenses, update the buffer calculation, check debt progress. (1 hour)
