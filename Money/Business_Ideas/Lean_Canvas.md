# Lean Canvas

> *A fast, one-page business plan framework for new ideas.*
> **Disclaimer:** This is for planning and experimentation, not a financial recommendation or guaranteed outcome.

## 1. Problem
*Top 1-3 problems your target audience faces.*

## 2. Customer Segments
*Target customers and users. Who are the early adopters?*

## 3. Unique Value Proposition
*Single, clear, compelling message that states why you are different and worth paying attention to.*

## 4. Solution
*Top 1-3 features of your product/service.*

## 5. Channels
*Path to customers. How will you reach them?*

## 6. Revenue Streams
*Revenue model, lifetime value, revenue, gross margin.*

## 7. Cost Structure
*Customer acquisition costs, distribution costs, hosting, people, etc.*

## 8. Key Metrics
*Key activities you measure.*

## 9. Unfair Advantage
*Can't be easily copied or bought.*
