# Lean Canvas Framework

> **Disclaimer:** This content is for planning and educational purposes only. It is not a financial recommendation or guarantee of business success.

## 1. Problem
- What are the top 1-3 problems your target audience faces?
- *Existing Alternatives:* How are they solving these problems today?

## 2. Customer Segments
- Who are your target customers and users?
- *Early Adopters:* Who are the very first people who will desperately want this?

## 3. Unique Value Proposition
- Single, clear, compelling message that states why you are different and worth paying attention to.
- *High-Level Concept:* X for Y (e.g., "YouTube for Podcasts").

## 4. Solution
- What are the top 1-3 features of your solution? (Keep it simple, how does it solve the problem?)

## 5. Channels
- How will you reach your customers? (e.g., SEO, Twitter, Newsletter sponsorships, direct outreach)

## 6. Revenue Streams
- What is the revenue model?
- What are the pricing tactics?
- How much are customers willing to pay?

## 7. Cost Structure
- What are your customer acquisition costs (CAC)?
- What are your distribution costs?
- What are your fixed and variable costs (e.g., hosting, software, labor)?

## 8. Key Metrics
- What are the key numbers that tell you how your business is doing? (e.g., signups, daily active users, MRR, churn rate)

## 9. Unfair Advantage
- What is something that cannot be easily copied or bought by competitors? (e.g., insider information, strong personal brand, existing audience, network effects)
