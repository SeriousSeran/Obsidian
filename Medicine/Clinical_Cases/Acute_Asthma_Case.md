# Deidentified Clinical Case: Acute Severe Asthma

> Not final clinical advice. Learning material only.

## Presentation
- 25-year-old female presents with acute shortness of breath and wheezing.
- Known history of asthma.
- Unable to complete sentences in one breath.

## Findings
- HR 115 bpm, RR 28/min, SpO2 93% on room air.
- Bilateral widespread polyphonic wheeze.
- No cyanosis.

## Assessment
- Acute severe asthma exacerbation.

## Learning Points
- Recognize criteria for moderate, severe, and life-threatening asthma.
- Initial management: Oxygen, nebulized salbutamol, ipratropium bromide, systemic corticosteroids.

## Follow Up
- Monitor peak flow.
- Review inhaler technique and trigger avoidance.
