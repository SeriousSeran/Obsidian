# Deidentified Clinical Case: Asthma Exacerbation

> Not final clinical advice. Learning material only.

## Presentation
- 25-year-old presenting with acute severe breathlessness and wheeze. Known asthmatic.

## Findings
- Widespread polyphonic wheeze, respiratory rate 28, oxygen saturation 92% on room air, unable to complete sentences.

## Assessment
- Acute severe asthma exacerbation.

## Learning Points
- Recognizing signs of life-threatening asthma (e.g., silent chest, cyanosis, poor respiratory effort).
