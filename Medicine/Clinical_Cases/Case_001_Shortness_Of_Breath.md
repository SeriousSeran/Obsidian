# Deidentified Clinical Case: Shortness of Breath

> Not final clinical advice. Learning material only.

## Presentation
A patient presenting with acute worsening of shortness of breath. History of smoking and previous similar episodes.

## Findings
Tachypnoea, use of accessory muscles, prolonged expiratory wheeze on auscultation. Oxygen saturation decreased on room air.

## Assessment
Acute exacerbation of chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD) or acute severe asthma.

## Learning Points
- Differentiating between COPD exacerbation and asthma based on history and reversibility.
- Importance of ABG in assessing respiratory failure (Type 1 vs Type 2).
