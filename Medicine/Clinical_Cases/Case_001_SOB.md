# Deidentified Clinical Case: Shortness of Breath

> Not final clinical advice. Learning material only.

## Presentation
65-year-old patient presents with acute onset shortness of breath and wheeze. History of smoking.

## Findings
Tachypneic, bilateral expiratory wheeze, prolonged expiratory phase. O2 sats 92% on room air.

## Assessment
Acute exacerbation of Asthma or COPD.

## Learning Points
- Always check oxygen saturations and respiratory rate first.
- Differentiate between asthma and COPD exacerbations based on history and reversibility.
