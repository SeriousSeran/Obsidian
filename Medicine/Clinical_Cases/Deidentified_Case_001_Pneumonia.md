# Deidentified Clinical Case

> Not final clinical advice. Learning material only.

## Presentation
65-year-old male presenting with a 3-day history of productive cough, fever, and shortness of breath. No significant past medical history.

## Findings
On respiratory examination, reduced chest expansion on the right side. Dullness to percussion over the right lower zone. Bronchial breath sounds and coarse crackles heard in the right lower zone.

## Assessment
Community-acquired pneumonia (CAP), likely bacterial.

## Learning Points
- CURB-65 score is a useful tool for assessing the severity of CAP.
- Typical signs of consolidation: dullness to percussion, bronchial breath sounds, increased vocal resonance.
