# Deidentified Clinical Case: Case 001 Community Acquired Pneumonia

> Not final clinical advice. Learning material only.

## Presentation
65-year-old male presenting with a 3-day history of productive cough (rusty sputum), fever, and right-sided pleuritic chest pain.

## Findings
- **Vitals:** T 38.5C, HR 110, RR 24, BP 100/60, SpO2 92% on RA.
- **Examination:** Right lower zone bronchial breathing, coarse crackles, and dull percussion note.

## Assessment
Community Acquired Pneumonia (CAP). CURB-65 score is 2 (Age > 65, Urea pending, RR > 30 = No, BP < 90/60 = No, Confusion = No). Actually RR is 24. So CURB-65 is 1 based on clinical features alone.

## Learning Points
- CURB-65 scoring is vital for determining the appropriate location of care and severity (Confusion, Urea > 7, RR >= 30, BP < 90/60, Age >= 65).
- Typical organisms include Streptococcus pneumoniae, Haemophilus influenzae.
