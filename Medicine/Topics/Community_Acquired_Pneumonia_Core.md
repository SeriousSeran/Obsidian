# Community Acquired Pneumonia Core

> **Domain:** Medicine | **Status:** Active | **Review:** Pending
> Not final clinical advice. Learning material only.

---

## Definition

> What is this condition/concept in one clear sentence?

**Definition ::** Infection of the lung parenchyma acquired outside of a hospital or healthcare setting.

---

## Pathophysiology 🔬

> Mechanism — what goes wrong at the cellular or physiological level?

**Pathophysiology ::** Pathogens enter the lower respiratory tract, bypassing host defenses, leading to alveolar inflammation, consolidation, and impaired gas exchange.

---

## Clinical Features 🩺

### Symptoms
- Fever
- Cough (often productive)
- Pleuritic chest pain
- Dyspnea
- Sputum production

### Signs
- Tachypnea
- Tachycardia
- Hypoxia
- Focal crackles or bronchial breathing
- Dullness to percussion

### Investigations
| Test | Expected Finding | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Chest X-ray | Focal consolidation | To confirm diagnosis and rule out other causes |
| Sputum Culture | Identifies pathogen (e.g. Strep pneumoniae) | To target antibiotic therapy |
| Blood Cultures | May be positive in severe cases | To identify systemic spread |
| FBC/CRP | Elevated WCC and CRP | Signs of infection/inflammation |

**Key investigation ::** Chest X-ray.

---

## Diagnosis

**Gold standard:**
> Chest X-ray showing new infiltrate with clinical signs of infection.

**Gold standard investigation ::** Chest X-ray.

**Clinical criteria / scoring:**
- CURB-65 score (Confusion, Urea >7, Respiratory rate >=30, Blood pressure <90/60, Age >=65) to assess severity and mortality risk.

---

## Management 💊

### Acute
1. Oxygen therapy if hypoxic.
2. Empirical antibiotics based on severity and local guidelines.
3. IV fluids if hypotensive or dehydrated.

### Long-term
- Smoking cessation advice.
- Pneumococcal and influenza vaccination in susceptible groups.

### Medications
| Drug | Dose | Indication | Key Side Effects |
|---|---|---|---|
| Amoxicillin | Varies | Mild CAP (typical first line) | GI upset, rash |
| Doxycycline / Macrolide | Varies | Atypical coverage | Photosensitivity (doxy), QT prolongation (macrolide) |

**First-line treatment ::** Amoxicillin (mild CAP).

---

## Complications ⚠️

- Parapneumonic effusion
- Empyema
- Sepsis
- Respiratory failure
- Lung abscess

**Most serious complication ::** Sepsis/respiratory failure.

---

## Viva Points 🎯

> What examiners love to ask about this topic.

**Viva Q — Classic presentation ::** Older patient with fever, productive cough, pleuritic chest pain, and focal crackles.

**Viva Q — Why this investigation? ::** Chest X-ray confirms consolidation and helps rule out other causes like heart failure or malignancy.

**Viva Q — Management priority ::** Early administration of appropriate antibiotics and oxygen therapy.
