# Community Acquired Pneumonia

> Not final clinical advice. Learning material only.

## Definition

> Acute infection of the pulmonary parenchyma acquired in the community.

## Pathophysiology 🔬

> Microorganisms reach the lower respiratory tract, overwhelming alveolar macrophages, leading to an inflammatory response and alveolar exudate.

## Clinical Features 🩺

### Symptoms
- Cough (productive), fever, dyspnoea, pleuritic chest pain.

### Signs
- Tachypnoea, tachycardia, localized crackles, bronchial breathing, dullness to percussion.

## Diagnosis

**Gold standard investigation ::** Chest X-ray (showing consolidation).

**Clinical criteria / scoring:** CURB-65 score to assess severity and mortality risk.

## Management 💊

### Acute
1. Oxygen therapy if hypoxaemic.
2. Empirical antibiotics (e.g., Amoxicillin for low severity).
3. Intravenous fluids if hypotensive or severely dehydrated.

## Complications ⚠️

- Pleural effusion, empyema, lung abscess, sepsis.

## Viva Points 🎯

**Viva Q — What is the CURB-65 criteria? ::** Confusion, Urea >7 mmol/L, Respiratory rate >=30, Blood pressure <90 systolic or <=60 diastolic, Age >=65.
