# Asthma

> **Domain:** Medicine | **Status:** Draft | **Review:** Pending
> Not final clinical advice. Learning material only.

---

## Definition

> What is this condition/concept in one clear sentence?

**Definition ::** Chronic inflammatory airway disease characterized by reversible airway obstruction and bronchial hyperresponsiveness.

---

## Pathophysiology 🔬

> Mechanism — what goes wrong at the cellular or physiological level?

**Pathophysiology ::** Inflammation, airway remodeling, and smooth muscle contraction leading to narrowed airways.

---

## Clinical Features 🩺

### Symptoms
- Wheeze, shortness of breath, chest tightness, cough (often worse at night).

### Signs
- Polyphonic expiratory wheeze, hyperinflated chest, use of accessory muscles in severe attacks.

### Investigations
| Test | Expected Finding | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Spirometry | Obstructive pattern (FEV1/FVC < 0.7) | Reversible with bronchodilators |

**Key investigation ::** Bedside peak expiratory flow rate (PEFR).

---

## Management 💊

### Acute
1. Oxygen (if hypoxic).
2. Salbutamol nebulizers.
3. Oral/IV steroids (Prednisolone/Hydrocortisone).

**First-line treatment ::** Inhaled Corticosteroids (ICS) + Long-Acting Beta Agonist (LABA) for maintenance.

---

## Complications ⚠️

**Most serious complication ::** Life-threatening acute severe asthma (status asthmaticus).
