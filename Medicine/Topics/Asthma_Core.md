# Asthma Core

> Not final clinical advice. Learning material only.

## Core Concept
- Chronic inflammatory disease of the airways causing reversible airflow obstruction.

## Clinical Pattern
- Wheeze, breathlessness, chest tightness, and cough (often worse at night or early morning).

## OSCE/Viva Hooks
- Link to [[Medicine/OSCE/Respiratory_Examination_Station]].
- Link to [[Medicine/Viva/Viva_Question_Bank]] for "Causes of wheeze?".

## Differentials
- COPD
- Heart failure (cardiac wheeze)
- Anaphylaxis
- Foreign body aspiration

## Red Flags
- Silent chest, cyanosis, poor respiratory effort.
- Peak flow <33% of best or predicted.
- SpO2 < 92%.
