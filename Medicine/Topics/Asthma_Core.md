# Asthma Core

> Not final clinical advice. Learning material only.

## Definition
A chronic inflammatory airway disease characterized by reversible airflow obstruction, bronchial hyperresponsiveness, and airway inflammation.

## Pathophysiology 🔬
Airway inflammation leads to mucosal edema, increased mucus production, and smooth muscle contraction (bronchospasm), causing airflow limitation, particularly during expiration.

## Clinical Features 🩺
### Symptoms
- Wheeze
- Breathlessness
- Chest tightness
- Cough (often worse at night or early morning)

### Signs
- Widespread, bilateral, polyphonic wheeze
- Tachypnea
- Tachycardia

## Management 💊
### Acute
1. Oxygen (if hypoxemic)
2. Short-acting beta-agonists (SABA) via inhaler or nebulizer (e.g., Salbutamol)
3. Systemic corticosteroids (Oral Prednisolone or IV Hydrocortisone)

### Long-term
- Inhaled corticosteroids (ICS)

## 📇 Flashcards
#flashcards

**What is the classic presentation of asthma? ::** Wheeze, breathlessness, chest tightness, and cough (often nocturnal).

**What is the first-line acute treatment for an asthma exacerbation? ::** SABA (e.g., Salbutamol) and systemic corticosteroids.
