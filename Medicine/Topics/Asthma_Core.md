# Asthma Core

> Not final clinical advice. Learning material only.

## Core Concept
- Chronic inflammatory disorder of the airways characterized by reversible airflow obstruction and bronchospasm.

## Clinical Features
- Wheeze, breathlessness, chest tightness, and cough, varying over time and in intensity.

## OSCE/Viva Hooks
- Link to [[Medicine/OSCE/Respiratory_Examination_Station]].
- Link to [[Medicine/Viva/Viva_Question_Bank]] for "Causes of wheeze?".

## Management
- Avoid triggers, inhaled corticosteroids (ICS), short-acting beta-agonists (SABA), leukotriene receptor antagonists.
