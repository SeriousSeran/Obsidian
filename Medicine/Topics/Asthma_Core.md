# Asthma Core

> Not final clinical advice. Learning material only.

## Core Concept
- Chronic inflammatory disease of the airways causing reversible airflow obstruction and bronchospasm.

## Clinical Pattern
- Wheeze, shortness of breath, chest tightness, and cough, typically worse at night or early morning. Variable expiratory airflow limitation.

## Differentials
- COPD
- Heart failure (cardiac wheeze)
- Anaphylaxis
- Foreign body aspiration
- Airway obstruction

## Red Flags
- Silent chest
- Cyanosis
- Exhaustion / confusion
- Peak expiratory flow < 33% of best or predicted
