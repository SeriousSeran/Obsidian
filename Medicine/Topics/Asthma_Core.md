# Asthma Core

> Not final clinical advice. Learning material only.

## Core Concept
- A chronic inflammatory disorder of the airways characterized by reversible airflow obstruction and airway hyper-responsiveness.

## Clinical Pattern
- Intermittent wheeze, breathlessness, chest tightness, and cough, often worse at night or early morning.

## Differentials
- COPD
- Heart failure
- Bronchiectasis
- Pulmonary embolism

## Red Flags
- Silent chest
- Cyanosis
- Poor respiratory effort
- Exhaustion, confusion
