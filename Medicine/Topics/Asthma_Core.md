# Asthma Core

> Not final clinical advice. Learning material only.

## Definition
Chronic inflammatory disorder of the airways characterized by reversible airflow obstruction and bronchospasm.

## Clinical Features 🩺
- Wheeze, breathlessness, chest tightness, and cough.
- Symptoms often worse at night or early morning.

## Diagnosis
- Spirometry: Obstructive pattern with significant reversibility.
- Peak expiratory flow (PEF) monitoring.

## Management 💊
- Acute: SABA, systemic corticosteroids, oxygen.
- Long-term: ICS, LABA.
