# Asthma Core

> Not final clinical advice. Learning material only.

## Definition

Chronic inflammatory disease of the airways causing reversible airflow obstruction and bronchial hyperresponsiveness.

---

## Pathophysiology 🔬

Triggered inflammation leads to bronchospasm, mucosal edema, and mucus plugging, narrowing airways.

---

## Clinical Features 🩺

### Symptoms
- Wheeze, dyspnea, chest tightness, cough (often worse at night or early morning).

### Signs
- Expiratory polyphonic wheeze, tachypnea, prolonged expiratory phase.

### Investigations
| Test | Expected Finding | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Spirometry | Obstructive pattern (FEV1/FVC < 0.7), significant reversibility with bronchodilator | To confirm diagnosis |

**Key investigation ::** Spirometry

---

## Diagnosis

**Gold standard:**
> Spirometry with bronchodilator reversibility testing.

**Clinical criteria / scoring:**
- Symptoms variable over time and in intensity.

---

## Management 💊

### Acute
1. Oxygen (if hypoxemic).
2. SABA (e.g., salbutamol) via spacer or nebulizer.
3. Systemic corticosteroids (e.g., prednisolone or hydrocortisone).
4. Consider ipratropium, magnesium sulfate in severe cases.

### Long-term
- Avoid triggers. Ensure good inhaler technique. Follow a stepwise approach.

### Medications
| Drug | Dose | Indication | Key Side Effects |
|---|---|---|---|
| SABA (Salbutamol) | PRN | Symptom relief | Tachycardia, tremor |
| ICS | Daily | Controller/preventer | Oral thrush (rinse mouth) |

**First-line treatment ::** Inhaled corticosteroids (ICS) combined with a long-acting beta-agonist (LABA) in many modern guidelines (e.g. MART approach), or SABA PRN with ICS.

---

## Complications ⚠️

- Status asthmaticus, respiratory failure, pneumothorax.

**Most serious complication ::** Acute severe or life-threatening asthma leading to respiratory arrest.

---

## Viva Points 🎯

**Viva Q — Classic presentation ::** Young patient with intermittent wheeze and nocturnal cough triggered by cold air or exercise.

**Viva Q — Why this investigation? ::** Spirometry demonstrates reversible obstruction, distinguishing it from fixed obstruction like COPD.

**Viva Q — Management priority ::** In acute settings: oxygenation and bronchodilation. In chronic: reducing airway inflammation with ICS.
