# Pneumonia

> Not final clinical advice. Learning material only.

---

## Definition
Pneumonia is an acute inflammatory response to infection in the lung parenchyma, leading to alveolar consolidation.

---

## Pathophysiology
Inhalation or aspiration of pathogens overrides the respiratory tract's defense mechanisms. This leads to an inflammatory response in the alveoli, resulting in exudate filling the alveolar spaces and impairing gas exchange (consolidation).

---

## Clinical Features

### Symptoms
- Fever, chills, rigors
- Productive cough (purulent or rust-colored sputum)
- Dyspnea
- Pleuritic chest pain

### Signs
- Tachypnea, tachycardia
- Hypoxia
- Bronchial breath sounds
- Coarse crackles
- Dullness to percussion
- Increased tactile vocal fremitus

---

## Diagnosis
**Gold standard:** Chest X-ray (CXR) showing new consolidation/infiltrate.

**Other investigations:**
- Sputum culture and gram stain
- Blood cultures (if severe)
- FBC (leukocytosis), CRP (elevated)
- U&Es (to assess for acute kidney injury, calculate CURB-65)

---

## Management

### Acute Assessment
Calculate **CURB-65** score to guide care setting:
- **C**onfusion (AMT < 8)
- **U**rea > 7 mmol/L
- **R**espiratory Rate >= 30/min
- **B**lood Pressure (SBP < 90 or DBP <= 60)
- Age >= **65**

### Treatment
- **Mild (Score 0-1):** Outpatient. Oral amoxicillin (or macrolide if allergic).
- **Moderate (Score 2):** Inpatient admission. Dual therapy (e.g., amoxicillin + macrolide).
- **Severe (Score 3-5):** ICU assessment. IV antibiotics (e.g., co-amoxiclav + clarithromycin).
- Oxygen therapy to target SpO2 94-98% (or 88-92% in COPD patients).
- IV fluids if hypotensive or dehydrated.

---

## Complications
- Pleural effusion
- Empyema
- Lung abscess
- Sepsis / Septic shock
- Respiratory failure

---

## Viva Points

- **What are the common atypical organisms?** Mycoplasma pneumoniae, Legionella pneumophila, Chlamydophila pneumoniae.
- **When is a repeat CXR indicated?** Typically 6 weeks after treatment to ensure resolution, especially in patients >50 years old or smokers, to exclude an underlying endobronchial lesion like lung cancer.

---

## OSCE Tips
- In a respiratory exam, explicitly comment on the nature of breath sounds (vesicular vs. bronchial) and whether adventitious sounds like crackles clear with coughing.
- Always offer to check O2 saturation and perform a full set of observations.
