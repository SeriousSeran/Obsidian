# Pneumonia Core

> Not final clinical advice. Learning material only.

## Core Concept
- An acute lower respiratory tract infection characterized by inflammation of the lung parenchyma, usually caused by bacteria, viruses, or fungi. It presents with new focal chest signs, fever, and radiographic evidence of consolidation.

## Clinical Pattern
- Fever, rigors, productive cough (purulent or rusty sputum), pleuritic chest pain, dyspnoea, tachypnoea.
- Signs: focal coarse crackles, bronchial breathing, dullness to percussion, increased vocal resonance.

## Differentials
- Heart failure / Pulmonary oedema
- Pulmonary embolism
- Exacerbation of COPD or Asthma
- Lung cancer (can present with post-obstructive pneumonia)

## Red Flags
- Confusion (new onset)
- Urea > 7 mmol/L
- Respiratory rate >= 30/min
- Blood pressure (Systolic < 90 mmHg or Diastolic <= 60 mmHg)
- Age >= 65 years
- (CURB-65 criteria indicating severe pneumonia and need for admission/ICU)
