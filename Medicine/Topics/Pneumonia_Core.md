# Pneumonia Core

> Not final clinical advice. Learning material only.

## Core Concept
- Inflammation of the lung parenchyma, typically caused by an infection. Characterized by consolidation of the affected part of the lung.

## Clinical Pattern
- Cough (often productive of purulent or rusty sputum), fever, shortness of breath, pleuritic chest pain, systemic signs of infection (tachycardia, tachypnoea, hypoxia).

## Differentials
- Heart failure
- COPD exacerbation
- Pulmonary embolism
- Asthma exacerbation

## Red Flags
- Severe hypoxia or respiratory failure
- Shock or severe sepsis
- Confusion (especially in the elderly)
