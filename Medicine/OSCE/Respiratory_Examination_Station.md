# Respiratory Examination Station

> OSCE station map for respiratory examination. Not final clinical advice.

## Opening
- Identify patient, wash hands, explain, consent. Position patient at 45 degrees. Expose chest adequately.

## Focused task
- Inspect: general appearance, hands (clubbing, tremor), face (cyanosis), neck (JVP), chest (scars, shape, symmetry of expansion).
- Palpate: tracheal position, cricosternal distance, apex beat, chest expansion, tactile vocal fremitus.
- Percuss: lung fields (anterior, lateral, posterior), supraclavicular fossae, axillae. Check for dullness or hyperresonance.
- Auscultate: breath sounds (vesicular, bronchial), added sounds (wheeze, crackles, rub), vocal resonance.

## Findings
- State present and absent clinical findings plainly. E.g., "On auscultation, there was widespread polyphonic wheeze with prolonged expiration."

## Differentials
- Consider asthma, COPD, pneumonia, heart failure, pulmonary fibrosis.

## Plan
- Investigations: Oxygen saturation, ABG, Chest X-ray, Peak expiratory flow rate.
- Review and escalation as necessary.
