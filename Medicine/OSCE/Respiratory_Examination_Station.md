# Respiratory Examination Station

> OSCE station map for respiratory examination. Not final clinical advice.

## Opening
- Identify patient, wash hands, explain, consent.
- Expose the patient appropriately (chest fully exposed, maintaining dignity).

## Focused task
- Inspect: general appearance, work of breathing, sputum pot, hands (clubbing, cyanosis, tremor), face (conjunctival pallor), neck (JVP, lymph nodes).
- Palpate: tracheal tug/deviation, cricosternal distance, chest expansion (anterior/posterior).
- Percuss: lung fields (anterior, lateral, posterior), mapping any dullness or hyperresonance.
- Auscultate: breath sounds, vocal resonance. Identify added sounds (wheeze, crackles, rub).

## Findings
- State present and absent clinical findings plainly (e.g., "Symmetrical chest expansion, normal vesicular breath sounds with no added sounds").

## Differentials
- Consider asthma, COPD, pneumonia, pleural effusion, pneumothorax, pulmonary fibrosis.

## Plan
- Investigations: Oxygen saturation (SpO2), Peak Expiratory Flow (PEF), Arterial Blood Gas (ABG), Chest X-Ray.
- Review and escalation as necessary.
