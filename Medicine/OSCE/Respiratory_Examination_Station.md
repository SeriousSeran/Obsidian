# Respiratory Examination Station

> Not final clinical advice. Learning material only.

## Opening
- Identify patient, wash hands, explain procedure, gain consent, ensure adequate exposure (chest).

## Focused task
- **Inspect**: general appearance, hands (clubbing, tar staining), face (pallor, cyanosis), chest (scars, deformity, respiratory effort).
- **Palpate**: tracheal deviation, chest expansion, apex beat.
- **Percuss**: lung fields (dullness, hyper-resonance).
- **Auscultate**: breath sounds (vesicular, bronchial), added sounds (wheeze, crackles), vocal resonance.

## Findings
- State present and absent clinical findings plainly.
- E.g., "On examination of the chest, there was bilateral widespread polyphonic expiratory wheeze with a prolonged expiratory phase."

## Differentials
- Consider asthma, COPD exacerbation, pneumonia, heart failure, bronchiectasis.

## Plan
- Investigations: Oxygen saturations, peak flow, ABG (if severe), chest X-ray.
- Management: As per findings (e.g., bronchodilators, steroids, oxygen, antibiotics).
- Escalation: Senior review if the patient is unstable.
