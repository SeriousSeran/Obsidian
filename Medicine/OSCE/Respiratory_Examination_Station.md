# Respiratory Examination Station

> OSCE station map for respiratory examination. Not final clinical advice.

## Opening
- Identify patient, wash hands, explain, consent.

## Focused task
- Inspect: general appearance, sputum pots, hands (clubbing, tremor), face (cyanosis, Horner's).
- Palpate: tracheal position, cricosternal distance, apex beat, chest expansion.
- Percuss: lung fields (anterior, lateral, posterior), clavicles.
- Auscultate: breath sounds, added sounds (wheeze, crackles), vocal resonance.

## Findings
- State present and absent clinical findings plainly.

## Differentials
- Consider asthma, COPD, pneumonia, pulmonary effusion, fibrosis.

## Plan
- Investigations: Peak flow, ABG, Chest X-ray.
- Review and escalation as necessary.
