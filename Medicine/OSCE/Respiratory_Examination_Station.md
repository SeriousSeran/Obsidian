# Respiratory Examination Station

> Not final clinical advice. Learning material only.

## Opening
- Identify patient, wash hands, explain, consent.
- Position the patient at 45 degrees, adequately exposed to the waist.

## Focused task
- **Inspect:** General appearance, respiratory distress, sputum pots, oxygen, hands (clubbing, cyanosis, tremor), face (conjunctival pallor, central cyanosis).
- **Palpate:** Tracheal deviation, cricosternal distance, chest expansion, tactile vocal fremitus.
- **Percuss:** Lung fields (anterior, lateral, posterior), comparing left to right.
- **Auscultate:** Vesicular vs bronchial breathing, added sounds (wheeze, crackles, rub), vocal resonance.

## Findings
- State present and absent clinical findings plainly.
- E.g., "The patient has a symmetrical chest with normal expansion, vesicular breath sounds throughout, and no added sounds."

## Differentials
- Consider asthma, COPD exacerbation, pneumonia, pulmonary effusion, pulmonary fibrosis.

## Plan
- Investigations: Sputum culture, chest X-ray, ABG (if indicated).
- Review and escalation as necessary.
