# Respiratory Examination Station

> OSCE station map for respiratory examination. Not final clinical advice.

## Opening
- Identify patient, wash hands, explain, consent.
- Expose the chest appropriately.

## Focused task
- **Inspect**: general appearance, respiratory rate, cyanosis, clubbing, chest wall shape.
- **Palpate**: tracheal position, cricosternal distance, chest expansion, apex beat.
- **Percuss**: anterior, lateral, and posterior lung fields (comparing sides).
- **Auscultate**: breath sounds (vesicular or bronchial), added sounds (wheeze, crackles, rub), vocal resonance.

## Findings
- State present and absent clinical findings plainly.

## Differentials
- Consider asthma, COPD, pneumonia, pleural effusion, pneumothorax.

## Plan
- Investigations: Oxygen saturation, PEFR, Chest X-Ray, Sputum culture.
- Review and escalation as necessary.
