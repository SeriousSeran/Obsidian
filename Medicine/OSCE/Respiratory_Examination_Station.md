# Respiratory Examination Station

> OSCE station map for respiratory examination. Not final clinical advice. Learning material only.

## Opening
- Identify patient, wash hands, explain, consent.

## Focused task
- Inspect: general appearance, hands, face, neck, chest shape.
- Palpate: trachea, expansion, tactile vocal fremitus.
- Percuss: lung fields (anterior, lateral, posterior).
- Auscultate: breath sounds, added sounds, vocal resonance.

## Findings
- State present and absent clinical findings plainly.

## Differentials
- Consider asthma, COPD, pneumonia, pleural effusion.

## Plan
- Investigations: Peak flow, Sputum culture, Chest X-ray.
- Review and escalation as necessary.
