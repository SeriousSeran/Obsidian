# Respiratory Examination Station

> Not final clinical advice. Learning material only.

## Station Brief

You are a junior doctor in the respiratory clinic. Please perform a full respiratory examination on this patient.

## Inspection

- **General Inspection:** Oxygen therapy, inhalers, sputum pots, breathlessness, cachexia.
- **Hands:** Clubbing, tar staining, peripheral cyanosis, tremor (beta-agonists).
- **Face/Neck:** Central cyanosis, JVP (cor pulmonale), Horner's syndrome.

## Palpation

- Tracheal position.
- Chest expansion.
- Apex beat.
- Tactile vocal fremitus.

## Percussion

- Assess all lung zones (resonant, hyper-resonant, dull, stony dull).

## Auscultation

- Vesicular breath sounds vs. bronchial breathing.
- Added sounds (wheeze, crackles, rub).
- Vocal resonance.

## Conclusion

- Summarise findings succinctly.
- Suggest completing the examination by checking oxygen saturations, observing a sputum sample, and requesting a chest X-ray.
