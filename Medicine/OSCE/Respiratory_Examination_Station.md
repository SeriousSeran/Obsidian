# Respiratory Examination Station

> OSCE station map for respiratory examination. Not final clinical advice. Learning material only.

## Opening
- Identify patient, wash hands, explain, consent.
- Position at 45 degrees, expose chest appropriately.

## Focused task
- Inspect: sputum pot, inhalers, oxygen, work of breathing, chest shape.
- Palpate: trachea, cricosternal distance, apex beat, chest expansion.
- Percuss: lung fields anteriorly, laterally, and posteriorly.
- Auscultate: breath sounds, added sounds (wheeze, crackles), vocal resonance.

## Findings
- State present and absent clinical findings plainly.

## Differentials
- Asthma, COPD, pneumonia, pleural effusion, pneumothorax.

## Plan
- Investigations: Sputum culture, Chest X-ray, ABG (if indicated).
- Review and escalation as necessary.
