# Respiratory Examination Station

> OSCE station map for respiratory examination. Not final clinical advice. Learning material only.

## Opening
- Identify patient, wash hands, explain procedure, gain consent.

## Focused task
- Inspect: general appearance, work of breathing, cyanosis, clubbing, inhalers around bedside.
- Palpate: tracheal deviation, chest expansion, tactile vocal fremitus.
- Percuss: lung fields (anterior and posterior) for dullness or hyperresonance.
- Auscultate: breath sounds (vesicular vs bronchial), added sounds (wheeze, crackles), vocal resonance.

## Findings
- State present and absent clinical findings plainly. e.g., "On auscultation, there was widespread polyphonic wheeze."

## Differentials
- Asthma, COPD, Pneumonia, Pulmonary Fibrosis.

## Plan
- Investigations: Peak flow, Spirometry, Chest X-Ray, ABG if indicated.
- Review and escalation as necessary.
