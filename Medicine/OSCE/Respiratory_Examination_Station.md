# Respiratory Examination Station

> OSCE station map for respiratory examination. Not final clinical advice.

## Opening
- Identify patient, wash hands, explain, consent, position at 45 degrees.

## Focused task
- Inspect: sputum pot, inhalers, general appearance, hands (clubbing), face, chest shape.
- Palpate: trachea, cricosternal distance, apex beat, chest expansion, vocal fremitus.
- Percuss: anterior, lateral, and posterior chest.
- Auscultate: breath sounds, vocal resonance.

## Findings
- State present and absent clinical findings plainly (e.g., wheeze, crackles, dullness).

## Differentials
- Consider asthma, COPD, pneumonia, pleural effusion, pulmonary fibrosis.

## Plan
- Investigations: Peak flow, ABG, Chest X-ray.
- Review and escalation as necessary.
