# Respiratory Examination Station

> OSCE station map for respiratory examination. Not final clinical advice.

## Opening
- Identify patient, wash hands, explain, consent, position at 45 degrees, adequately expose chest.

## Focused task
- Inspect: general appearance, surroundings (inhalers, O2, sputum pot), hands (clubbing, cyanosis, tremor), face (conjunctival pallor), mouth (central cyanosis), neck (JVP, lymph nodes).
- Palpate: tracheal position, cricosternal distance, chest expansion, tactile vocal fremitus.
- Percuss: lung fields (anterior, lateral, posterior), supraclavicular fossa, axillae.
- Auscultate: breath sounds, added sounds (crackles, wheeze, rub), vocal resonance.

## Findings
- State present and absent clinical findings plainly (e.g., "On examination, there were reduced breath sounds and dullness to percussion at the right lung base").

## Differentials
- Consider consolidation (pneumonia), pleural effusion, pneumothorax, asthma, COPD, pulmonary fibrosis.

## Plan
- Investigations: Sputum sample, Peak Expiratory Flow Rate (PEFR), Oxygen saturation, Chest X-ray.
- Further examination: Cardiovascular examination, check calves for DVT.
