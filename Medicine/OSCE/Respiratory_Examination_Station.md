# Respiratory Examination Station

> OSCE station map for respiratory examination. Not final clinical advice. Learning material only.

## Opening
- Identify patient, wash hands, explain procedure, obtain consent.
- Position patient at 45 degrees, adequately exposed.

## Focused task

### Inspect
- **End of bed:** General appearance, work of breathing, accessory muscle use, supplemental oxygen, sputum pot.
- **Hands:** Clubbing, tar staining, peripheral cyanosis, flapping tremor (asterixis), pulse, respiratory rate.
- **Face/Neck:** Central cyanosis (tongue), conjunctival pallor, JVP, Horner's syndrome, cervical lymphadenopathy.
- **Chest:** Chest wall deformities (barrel chest, pectus excavatum/carinatum), scars.

### Palpate
- Tracheal deviation.
- Cricosternal distance.
- Apex beat (may be displaced).
- Chest expansion (anterior and posterior).
- Tactile vocal fremitus.

### Percuss
- Anterior, lateral, and posterior chest wall.
- Supraclavicular fossae.
- Note any dullness (consolidation, effusion) or hyper-resonance (pneumothorax).

### Auscultate
- Bell and diaphragm where appropriate, asking patient to breathe deeply through open mouth.
- Assess breath sounds (vesicular vs. bronchial).
- Note added sounds (wheeze, crackles, rubs).
- Vocal resonance (asking patient to say "99").

## Findings
- State present and absent clinical findings plainly.
- E.g., "The patient has a barrel chest, reduced chest expansion bilaterally, hyper-resonant percussion notes, and a widespread polyphonic wheeze, consistent with a background of obstructive airway disease."

## Differentials
- Asthma
- COPD
- Pneumonia
- Pleural effusion
- Pneumothorax

## Plan
- Investigations: Peak expiratory flow (PEF), oxygen saturations, ABG (if indicated), Chest X-ray, Sputum culture.
- Review and escalation as necessary.
