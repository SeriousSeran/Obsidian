# Respiratory Examination Station

> Not final clinical advice. Learning material only.
> OSCE station map for respiratory examination.

## Opening
- Identify patient, wash hands, explain, consent.

## Focused task
- Inspect: general appearance, sputum pot, oxygen, hands (clubbing, tremor), face, neck.
- Palpate: trachea, cricosternal distance, apex beat, chest expansion.
- Percuss: lung fields (anterior, lateral, posterior).
- Auscultate: lung fields for breath sounds (vesicular/bronchial), added sounds (wheeze, crackles), vocal resonance.

## Findings
- State present and absent clinical findings plainly.

## Differentials
- Consider asthma, COPD, pneumonia, pulmonary fibrosis, pleural effusion.

## Plan
- Investigations: Peak flow, ABG (if indicated), Chest X-ray, Sputum culture.
- Review and escalation as necessary.
