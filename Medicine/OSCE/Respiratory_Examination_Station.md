# Respiratory Examination Station

> Not final clinical advice. Learning material only.

## Opening
- Identify patient, wash hands, introduce self, explain the examination, and gain consent.
- Ensure the patient is adequately exposed (waist up) and positioned at 45 degrees.

## Focused task
- Inspect: general appearance (respiratory distress, cyanosis), hands (clubbing, tar staining), face, neck (JVP, tracheal tug).
- Palpate: trachea position, chest expansion, tactile vocal fremitus.
- Percuss: lung fields symmetrically (anterior, lateral, posterior).
- Auscultate: breath sounds symmetrically, vocal resonance.

## Findings
- State present and absent clinical findings plainly.

## Differentials
- Consider asthma, COPD, pneumonia, pleural effusion, pneumothorax.

## Plan
- Investigations: Peak flow, Spirometry, Sputum culture, Chest X-ray, ABG.
- Review and escalation as necessary.
