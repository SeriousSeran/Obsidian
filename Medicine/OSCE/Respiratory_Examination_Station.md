# Respiratory Examination Station

> Not final clinical advice. Learning material only.

## Opening
- Identify patient, wash hands, explain, consent.

## Focused task
- Inspect: sputum pot, inhalers, chest shape, respiratory rate.
- Palpate: tracheal tug, chest expansion, tactile vocal fremitus.
- Percuss: lung fields, clavicles.
- Auscultate: breath sounds, added sounds (wheeze, crackles), vocal resonance.

## Findings
- State present and absent clinical findings plainly.

## Differentials
- Consider asthma, COPD, pneumonia, pleural effusion.

## Plan
- Investigations: Peak flow, ABG, Chest X-ray.
- Review and escalation as necessary.
