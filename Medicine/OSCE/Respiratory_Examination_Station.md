# Respiratory Examination Station

> OSCE station map for respiratory examination. Not final clinical advice. Learning material only.

## Opening
- Identify patient, wash hands, explain procedure, gain consent.
- Position patient at 45 degrees, expose chest adequately.

## Focused task
- **Inspect (End of bed):** Respiratory rate, work of breathing, use of accessory muscles, cyanosis, inhalers, sputum pots.
- **Inspect (Hands & Face):** Clubbing, tar staining, tremor (beta-2 agonists), conjunctival pallor, central cyanosis.
- **Inspect (Chest):** Scars, deformities (barrel chest, pectus excavatum).
- **Palpate:** Tracheal deviation, cricosternal distance, chest expansion (anteriorly and posteriorly).
- **Percuss:** Clavicles, anterior chest, axillae, posterior chest (compare side-to-side).
- **Auscultate:** Breath sounds (vesicular, bronchial), added sounds (wheeze, crackles, rub), vocal resonance.

## Findings
- State present and absent clinical findings plainly (e.g., "The patient has a normal respiratory rate, symmetrical chest expansion, vesicular breath sounds, and no added crackles or wheeze.").

## Differentials
- Consolidation (Pneumonia)
- Pleural effusion
- COPD / Asthma
- Pulmonary fibrosis
- Pneumothorax

## Plan
- **Bedside:** Oxygen saturations, temperature, peak flow, sputum sample.
- **Investigations:** Chest X-ray, ABG (if saturations <92% or risk of type II respiratory failure).
- Review and escalate as necessary.
