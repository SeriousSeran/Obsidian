# Respiratory Examination Station

> OSCE station map for respiratory examination. Not final clinical advice.

## Opening
- Identify patient, wash hands, explain, consent.

## Focused task
- Inspect: sputum pot, inhalers, oxygen, general appearance (cyanosis, clubbing), chest wall deformities.
- Palpate: tracheal deviation, chest expansion.
- Percuss: lung fields (resonant, dull, stony dull).
- Auscultate: breath sounds (vesicular, bronchial), added sounds (wheeze, crackles, rub), vocal resonance.

## Findings
- State present and absent clinical findings plainly.

## Differentials
- Consider asthma, COPD, pneumonia, pleural effusion.

## Plan
- Investigations: Chest X-ray, Spirometry, Sputum culture.
