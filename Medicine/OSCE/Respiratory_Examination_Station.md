# Respiratory Examination Station

> Not final clinical advice. Learning material only.

## Opening
- Identify patient, wash hands, explain procedure, gain consent.
- Position patient at 45 degrees.
- Expose chest adequately.

## Focused task
- **Inspect**: General appearance, respiratory rate, effort, cyanosis, inhalers/sputum pot by bed.
- **Hands & Face**: Clubbing, flapping tremor (asterixis), pallor, ptosis/miosis (Horner's).
- **Palpate**: Tracheal deviation, chest expansion, tactile vocal fremitus.
- **Percuss**: Anterior, lateral, and posterior lung fields.
- **Auscultate**: Breath sounds (vesicular/bronchial), added sounds (wheeze, crackles), vocal resonance.
- **Lymph nodes**: Cervical and supraclavicular (e.g., Virchow's node).

## Findings
- State present and absent clinical findings plainly.
- E.g., "On examination, there was reduced expansion, stony dull percussion, and absent breath sounds at the right lung base, consistent with a pleural effusion."

## Differentials
- Asthma/COPD (polyphonic wheeze)
- Pneumonia (coarse crackles, bronchial breathing)
- Pulmonary fibrosis (fine end-inspiratory crackles)
- Pleural effusion (stony dullness, reduced breath sounds)

## Plan
- Investigations: Sputum culture, Chest X-ray (CXR), Spirometry/Peak Flow, Arterial Blood Gas (ABG) if hypoxic.
- Consider review and escalation if respiratory distress.
