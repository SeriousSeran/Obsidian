# Respiratory Examination Station

> OSCE station map for respiratory examination. Not final clinical advice. Learning material only.

## Opening
- Identify patient, wash hands, explain, consent.

## Focused task
- Inspect: general appearance, hands, face, neck, chest wall.
- Palpate: tracheal deviation, cricosternal distance, apex beat, chest expansion, tactile vocal fremitus.
- Percuss: lung fields (anterior and posterior), axillae.
- Auscultate: breath sounds, vocal resonance.

## Findings
- State present and absent clinical findings plainly.
- E.g., presence of wheeze, crackles, reduced breath sounds.

## Differentials
- Consider asthma, COPD, pneumonia, pleural effusion, pneumothorax.

## Plan
- Investigations: Oxygen saturation, chest X-ray, spirometry/peak flow, ABG (if indicated).
- Review and escalation as necessary.
