# Respiratory Examination Station

> Not final clinical advice. Learning material only.

## Opening
- Identify patient, wash hands, explain, consent.
- Ensure patient is appropriately positioned (usually 45 degrees).

## Focused task
- Inspect: general appearance, sputum pot, hands (clubbing, cyanosis, tremor), face (conjunctival pallor), neck (JVP).
- Palpate: tracheal deviation, chest expansion.
- Percuss: lung fields (resonant, dull, stony dull).
- Auscultate: breath sounds (vesicular, bronchial), added sounds (wheeze, crackles), vocal resonance.

## Findings
- State present and absent clinical findings plainly.
- E.g., "The patient has a polyphonic expiratory wheeze bilaterally with reduced air entry."

## Differentials
- Consider asthma, COPD, pneumonia, pulmonary fibrosis, pleural effusion, pneumothorax.

## Plan
- Investigations: Peak flow, ABG, Chest X-ray.
- Review and escalation as necessary.
