# Respiratory Examination Station

> Not final clinical advice. Learning material only.

## Opening
- Identify patient, wash hands, explain, consent.
- Position the patient at 45 degrees, exposing the chest.

## Focused task
- **Inspect**: General appearance, hands (clubbing, tremor, cyanosis), face, neck (JVP, trachea).
- **Palpate**: Tracheal deviation, chest expansion, apex beat, tactile vocal fremitus.
- **Percuss**: Anterior and posterior chest, assessing for dullness or hyper-resonance.
- **Auscultate**: Anterior and posterior chest for breath sounds (vesicular, bronchial), added sounds (wheeze, crackles), and vocal resonance.

## Findings
- State present and absent clinical findings plainly. e.g., "On auscultation, there was widespread polyphonic expiratory wheeze."

## Differentials
- Asthma, COPD, Pneumonia, Pulmonary Fibrosis, Pleural Effusion.

## Plan
- Investigations: Oxygen saturation, Sputum culture, Chest X-Ray, Spirometry.
- Review and escalation as necessary.
