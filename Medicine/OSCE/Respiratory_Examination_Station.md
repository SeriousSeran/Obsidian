# Respiratory Examination Station

> OSCE station map for respiratory examination. Not final clinical advice.

## Opening
- Identify patient, wash hands, explain, consent.

## Focused task
- Inspect: sputum pot, inhalers, hands (clubbing, cyanosis, flapping tremor), face (conjunctival pallor), neck (JVP, lymph nodes).
- Palpate: tracheal deviation, cricosternal distance, chest expansion, tactile vocal fremitus.
- Percuss: lung fields (clavicle to bases, axilla).
- Auscultate: breath sounds (vesicular vs bronchial), added sounds (wheeze, crackles), vocal resonance.

## Findings
- State present and absent clinical findings plainly. e.g., "The patient has clubbing and coarse late inspiratory crackles at the lung bases."

## Differentials
- Consider COPD, asthma, pneumonia, pulmonary fibrosis, pleural effusion.

## Plan
- Investigations: Sputum culture, chest X-ray (CXR), ABG, spirometry.
- Review and escalation as necessary.
