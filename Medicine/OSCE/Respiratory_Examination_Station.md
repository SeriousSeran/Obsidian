# Respiratory Examination Station

> OSCE station map for respiratory examination. Not final clinical advice. Learning material only.

## Opening
- Identify patient (Name, DOB).
- Wash hands.
- Explain procedure and gain consent.
- Ensure patient is appropriately exposed (chest to waist) and positioned (at 45 degrees).

## Focused task
- **Inspect:**
  - General: cyanosis, respiratory distress, accessory muscle use, cachexia.
  - Hands: clubbing, tar staining, peripheral cyanosis, flapping tremor (asterixis).
  - Face: pallor (conjunctivae), central cyanosis (tongue/lips).
  - Chest: shape (barrel chest, pectus excavatum/carinatum), scars.
- **Palpate:**
  - Tracheal tug/deviation.
  - Chest expansion (symmetry).
  - Apex beat.
- **Percuss:**
  - Anterior and posterior chest walls (resonant, dull, stony dull).
  - Tactile vocal fremitus (or vocal resonance during auscultation).
- **Auscultate:**
  - Vesicular vs bronchial breathing.
  - Added sounds: wheeze (polyphonic/monophonic), crackles (fine/coarse), pleural rub.
  - Vocal resonance.

## Findings
- State positive and negative findings clearly.
  - *Example:* "On examination, the patient was comfortable at rest. There was widespread polyphonic expiratory wheeze with no focal crackles or reduced breath sounds."

## Differentials
- Asthma
- COPD
- Pneumonia
- Pleural effusion
- Pulmonary fibrosis

## Plan
- **Investigations:**
  - Bedside: Sputum culture, peak expiratory flow rate (PEFR).
  - Bloods: FBC, CRP, ABG (if hypoxic).
  - Imaging: Chest X-ray.
- **Further Examination:**
  - Examine for cor pulmonale (JVP, peripheral edema).
