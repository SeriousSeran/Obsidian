# Respiratory Examination Station

> OSCE station map for respiratory examination. Not final clinical advice. Learning material only.

## Opening
- Identify patient, wash hands, explain procedure, gain consent.
- Position at 45 degrees, adequately exposed.

## Focused task
- **Inspect**: General appearance, respiratory distress, oxygen, inhalers, hands (clubbing, tar staining, tremor), face (cyanosis, Horner's), neck (JVP, trachea, nodes).
- **Palpate**: Tracheal position, cricosternal distance, chest expansion, tactile vocal fremitus.
- **Percuss**: Anterior, lateral, and posterior chest (resonant, dull, stony dull).
- **Auscultate**: Breath sounds (vesicular, bronchial), added sounds (wheeze, crackles, rub), vocal resonance.

## Findings
- State present and absent clinical findings clearly.

## Differentials
- Consider asthma, COPD, pneumonia, pleural effusion, pneumothorax, pulmonary fibrosis.

## Plan
- **Investigations**: O2 saturations, ABG, Chest X-ray, Spirometry/PFTs, Sputum culture.
- Review and escalation as necessary.
