# Respiratory Examination Station

> OSCE station map for respiratory examination. Not final clinical advice.

## Opening
- Identify patient, wash hands, explain procedure, gain consent.

## Focused task
- **Inspect**: General appearance, hands (clubbing, tremor), face (cyanosis, ptosis), neck (JVP, trachea).
- **Palpate**: Tracheal position, chest expansion, tactile vocal fremitus.
- **Percuss**: Anterior, lateral, and posterior chest (resonant vs dull vs hyper-resonant).
- **Auscultate**: Vesicular breathing vs bronchial breathing, added sounds (wheeze, crackles, rub), vocal resonance.

## Findings
- Present positive and negative findings concisely. Example: "Symmetrical chest expansion, normal resonant percussion, and vesicular breath sounds with no added sounds."

## Differentials
- Asthma, COPD, pneumonia, pleural effusion, pneumothorax, pulmonary fibrosis.

## Plan
- **Investigations**: Pulse oximetry, ABG (if low O2), Chest X-ray, Spirometry.
- **Next steps**: Sputum culture, discuss with senior.
