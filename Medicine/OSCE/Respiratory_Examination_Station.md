# Respiratory Examination Station

> Not final clinical advice. Learning material only.

## Station Aim
To perform a structured, comprehensive respiratory examination to identify signs of pulmonary or systemic disease.

## 1. Opening
- **WIPER:** Wash hands, Introduce self, Patient details (name/DOB), Explain procedure ("I'd like to examine your chest, which involves looking, feeling, tapping, and listening"), Request consent.
- **Positioning:** Patient sitting at 45 degrees, exposed from waist up.
- **Pain:** Ask if they have any pain before starting.

## 2. General Inspection
- **End of bed:** Look for respiratory distress, use of accessory muscles, cyanosis, pallor, cachexia, equipment (oxygen masks, inhalers, sputum pots, mobility aids).
- **Sputum pot:** Check for volume and color if present.

## 3. Hands and Arms
- **Nails:** Clubbing (schamroth's window sign), tar staining, peripheral cyanosis.
- **Palms:** Palmar erythema.
- **Wrists:** Assess pulse (rate and rhythm - e.g., tachycardia in severe asthma), CO2 retention flap (asterixis).
- **Arms:** Bruising (steroids).

## 4. Face and Neck
- **Face:** Plethoric complexion.
- **Eyes:** Conjunctival pallor (anaemia), Horner's syndrome (ptosis, miosis, anhidrosis - associated with apical lung tumor).
- **Mouth:** Central cyanosis (under tongue).
- **Neck:** JVP (elevated in cor pulmonale).

## 5. Chest - Anterior
- **Inspection:** Scars, chest wall deformities (barrel chest, pectus excavatum/carinatum).
- **Palpation:** Tracheal deviation, cricosternal distance (normally 3-4 fingers), apex beat, chest expansion (look for symmetry).
- **Percussion:** Tap over lung fields systematically (supraclavicular, upper, middle, lower zones, axillae). Listen for resonance vs. dullness (consolidation, fluid).
- **Auscultation:** Use the diaphragm. Listen over same areas. Assess breath sounds (vesicular, bronchial, absent/reduced). Note added sounds (wheeze, crackles, rub).
- **Vocal Resonance:** Say "99". Increased resonance suggests consolidation.

## 6. Chest - Posterior
- Ask patient to lean forward and cross arms.
- **Inspection:** Scars, deformities.
- **Palpation:** Chest expansion.
- **Percussion:** Systematically over posterior lung fields.
- **Auscultation:** Systematically over posterior lung fields. Check sacral oedema.
- **Vocal Resonance:** As above.

## 7. Lymph Nodes & Legs
- Palpate cervical and supraclavicular lymph nodes (Virchow's node).
- Inspect legs for peripheral oedema (DVT, cor pulmonale) and erythema nodosum.

## 8. Closing
- Thank the patient.
- Offer to help them dress.
- Wash hands.

## 9. Presenting Findings
- "This patient was comfortable at rest. On examination of the hands, there was no clubbing. The pulse was regular... On auscultation, there was a polyphonic expiratory wheeze..."
