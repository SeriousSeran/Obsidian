# Respiratory Examination Station

> OSCE station map for respiratory examination. Not final clinical advice. Learning material only.

## Opening
- Identify patient, wash hands, explain, consent.

## Focused task
- Inspect: general appearance, hands (clubbing), face, neck (JVP, trachea).
- Palpate: chest expansion, tactile vocal fremitus, apex beat.
- Percuss: lung fields (anterior, lateral, posterior).
- Auscultate: breath sounds, added sounds (wheeze, crackles), vocal resonance.

## Findings
- State present and absent clinical findings plainly.

## Differentials
- Consider asthma, COPD, pneumonia, pleural effusion, pneumothorax.

## Plan
- Investigations: Oxygen saturation, ABG, Chest X-ray.
- Review and escalation as necessary.
