# Respiratory Examination Station

> OSCE station map for respiratory examination. Not final clinical advice.

## Opening
- Identify patient, wash hands, explain, consent.

## Focused task
- Inspect: sputum pot, inhalers, chest wall shape, respiratory rate.
- Palpate: tracheal deviation, chest expansion, tactile vocal fremitus.
- Percuss: lung fields, clavicles, axillae.
- Auscultate: breath sounds, added sounds (wheeze, crepitations), vocal resonance.

## Findings
- State present and absent clinical findings plainly.

## Differentials
- Consider asthma, COPD, pneumonia, pleural effusion.

## Plan
- Investigations: Peak flow, Spirometry, CXR.
- Review and escalation as necessary.
