# Respiratory Examination Station

> OSCE station map for respiratory examination. Not final clinical advice.

## Opening
- Identify patient, wash hands, explain, consent, and position patient at 45 degrees.

## Focused task
- **Inspect**: General appearance, hands (clubbing, tremor), face (cyanosis, conjunctival pallor), chest wall deformities.
- **Palpate**: Tracheal deviation, cricosternal distance, chest expansion, tactile vocal fremitus.
- **Percuss**: Anterior, lateral, and posterior chest (resonant, dull, stony dull, hyperresonant).
- **Auscultate**: Breath sounds (vesicular, bronchial), added sounds (wheeze, crackles, rub), vocal resonance.

## Findings
- State present and absent clinical findings plainly (e.g., "On auscultation, there were widespread expiratory wheezes bilaterally, but no crackles.").

## Differentials
- Consider asthma, COPD, pneumonia, pleural effusion, or pulmonary fibrosis based on the findings.

## Plan
- Investigations: Peak flow, Spirometry, Sputum culture, Chest X-ray, ABG (if low SpO2).
- Review and escalation as necessary.
