# Respiratory Examination Station

> OSCE station map for respiratory examination. Not final clinical advice.

## Opening
- Identify patient, wash hands, explain, consent.
- Position the patient at 45 degrees.
- Ensure adequate exposure of the chest.

## Focused task
- Inspect: general appearance, hands (clubbing, tar staining), face (cyanosis, Horner's), neck (JVP, trachea position).
- Palpate: chest expansion, tactile vocal fremitus, apex beat.
- Percuss: lung fields (anterior, lateral, posterior), supraclavicular areas.
- Auscultate: lung fields for breath sounds (vesicular/bronchial), added sounds (wheeze, crackles), vocal resonance.

## Findings
- State present and absent clinical findings plainly.
- E.g., "The patient has clubbing and coarse inspiratory crackles at the lung bases, consistent with pulmonary fibrosis."

## Differentials
- Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (COPD)
- Asthma
- Pneumonia
- Pulmonary Fibrosis
- Pleural Effusion
- Pneumothorax

## Plan
- Investigations: Oxygen saturation (SpO2), chest X-ray (CXR), sputum culture, spirometry, ABG if hypoxic.
- Review and escalation as necessary.
