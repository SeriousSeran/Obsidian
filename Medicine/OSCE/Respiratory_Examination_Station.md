# Respiratory Examination Station

> OSCE station map for respiratory examination. Not final clinical advice.

## Opening
- Identify patient, wash hands, explain, consent.

## Focused task
- Inspect: general appearance, sputum pot, hands (clubbing, cyanosis, tremor), face, neck.
- Palpate: trachea, chest expansion, tactile vocal fremitus.
- Percuss: lung fields (anterior and posterior).
- Auscultate: breath sounds, added sounds (crackles, wheeze), vocal resonance.

## Findings
- State present and absent clinical findings plainly.

## Differentials
- Consider pneumonia, COPD exacerbation, asthma, pulmonary effusion, pulmonary fibrosis.

## Plan
- Investigations: Chest X-ray, ABG if hypoxic, Sputum culture.
- Review and escalation as necessary.
