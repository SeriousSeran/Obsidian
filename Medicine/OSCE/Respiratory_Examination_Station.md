# Respiratory Examination Station

> Not final clinical advice. Learning material only.

## Opening
- Identify patient, wash hands, explain, consent.
- Position the patient at 45 degrees. Expose appropriately.

## Focused task
- Inspect: general appearance, hands (clubbing, cyanosis, tar staining), face, neck.
- Palpate: tracheal deviation, chest expansion, apex beat.
- Percuss: lung zones (anterior and posterior), checking for dullness or hyper-resonance.
- Auscultate: lung zones (vesicular or bronchial breathing, crackles, wheezes), vocal resonance.

## Findings
- State present and absent clinical findings plainly.

## Differentials
- Consider asthma, COPD exacerbation, pneumonia, pleural effusion, pneumothorax.

## Plan
- Investigations: Peak flow, ABG (if indicated), Chest X-ray.
- Review and escalation as necessary.
