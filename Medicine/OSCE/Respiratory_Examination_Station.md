# Respiratory Examination Station

> Not final clinical advice. Learning material only.

## Opening
- Identify patient, wash hands, explain procedure, gain consent, position at 45 degrees, adequately expose chest.

## Focused task
- Inspect: sputum pot, inhalers, oxygen, general appearance, hands (clubbing, tar staining, tremor), face (cyanosis, Horner's), neck (JVP, tracheal tug).
- Palpate: tracheal deviation, cricosternal distance, apex beat, chest expansion.
- Percuss: lung fields (anterior, lateral, posterior).
- Auscultate: breath sounds, added sounds (wheeze, crackles, rub), vocal resonance.

## Findings
- State present and absent clinical findings plainly. E.g. "I found a prolonged expiratory wheeze bilaterally with no evidence of clubbing or cyanosis."

## Differentials
- Consider asthma, COPD, pneumonia, pulmonary fibrosis, pleural effusion.

## Plan
- Investigations: Peak flow, Spirometry, Chest X-ray, ABG if hypoxic.
- Review and escalation as necessary.
