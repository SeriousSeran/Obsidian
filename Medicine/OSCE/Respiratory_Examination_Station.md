# Respiratory Examination Station

> OSCE station map for respiratory examination. Not final clinical advice.

## Opening
- Identify patient, wash hands, explain, consent.
- Expose chest appropriately (usually to the waist).
- Position patient at 45 degrees.

## Focused task
- Inspect: general appearance (cyanosis, distress), hands (clubbing, tar staining), face (pallor), chest (shape, scars, symmetry of movement).
- Palpate: tracheal deviation, cricosternal distance, apex beat, chest expansion, tactile vocal fremitus.
- Percuss: lung fields (anterior, lateral, posterior), comparing left to right.
- Auscultate: breath sounds (vesicular/bronchial), added sounds (crackles, wheeze, rubs), vocal resonance.

## Findings
- State present and absent clinical findings plainly.
- E.g., "The patient has a barrel-shaped chest with hyperresonant percussion notes and bilateral expiratory wheeze."

## Differentials
- Consider asthma, COPD, pneumonia, pleural effusion, pneumothorax, pulmonary fibrosis.

## Plan
- Investigations: Oxygen saturation, ABG (if indicated), Chest X-ray, Spirometry.
- Review and escalation as necessary.
